    What a finished assessment looks like

    1. A topic or a paper goes in

      Type a research topic or attach a paper PDF, choose the report language, and optionally say whose decision the report is for.

    2. Evidence is gathered first

      Code retrieves and validates papers, patents and market sources, then freezes them. The agents reason only over that evidence, and the weighted score is computed by code, not by the model.

    3. A decision brief comes out

      A scorecard for TRL, MRL, patent strength, market accessibility and evidence confidence; a cited report; the main risks and a recommended first step. Export Markdown or PDF, or share the link.

    Evidence comes from public sources: papers from OpenAlex, Crossref, PubMed, arXiv and Semantic Scholar, patents from Google Patents, and market sources through the web search service you choose.

    Common questions

    How long is my data kept?

    You can delete any assessment from its page, or your whole account, at any time. An uploaded PDF is deleted as soon as its text has been extracted. Your API keys stay with your account, encrypted, until you remove them.

    Which languages does it support?

    The interface is in English and Simplified Chinese. A report can be written in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian or Hindi; left on “Auto language”, it follows the language of your topic.

    What is a decision context?

    An optional part of the form that says whose decision the report is for: the asset, the target application, the decision owner and the decision to make. With all four, the report may give a GO / NO-GO for that decision, bounded by its evidence. Without them the assessment still runs, as an orientation brief that claims no decision.

    Can I upload a paper?

    Yes: attach a PDF of up to 50 MB instead of typing a topic. Text selected from it is sent to the model provider you chose, to extract what the paper contributes. The PDF is deleted as soon as that succeeds, and an extracted summary that no assessment uses is deleted within 24 hours. Upload only papers you are allowed to send to that provider.

    How are the fees worked out?

    This service charges nothing and cannot refund a provider's charge. An assessment runs on your own model key and search key, and each provider bills you directly, by its own meter. The table above is what one assessment cost in model fees when it was measured here; searches are billed by the search service, and both search services have a free allowance.

    What if an assessment fails partway? Am I charged?

    Its page says why it stopped. If the report had been written, the page keeps it as a draft, marked as unreviewed or unscored. You can resume the run from its last checkpoint: it starts again at the step that failed and reuses what was saved before it, and it uses one more of today's runs. Model tokens your provider has already billed stay billed by that provider, as for any run: this service cannot refund them. If the run failed because the model service could not be reached, it does not count toward today's runs, up to three such runs a day per account.
