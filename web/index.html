    All sample reports

    1. A topic or a paper goes in

      Type a research topic or attach a paper PDF, choose the report language, and optionally say whose decision the report is for.

    2. Evidence is gathered first

      Code retrieves and validates papers, patents and market sources, then freezes them. The agents reason only over that evidence, and the weighted score is computed by code, not by the model.

    3. A decision brief comes out

      A scorecard for TRL, MRL, patent strength, market accessibility and evidence confidence; a cited report; the main risks and a recommended first step. Export Markdown or PDF, or share the link. A run takes about 15 minutes.

    Your keys, your bill

    Assessments run on your own API keys: one model key (DeepSeek, Qwen, OpenAI or Anthropic) and one search key (Tavily or Serper). One measured run used about 70,000 model tokens; your providers bill you directly for that, and this service adds nothing. Keys stay in your browser; this server never stores them.
