    All sample reports

    1. A topic or a paper goes in

      Type a research topic or attach a paper PDF, choose the report language, and optionally say whose decision the report is for.

    2. Evidence is gathered first

      Code retrieves and validates papers, patents and market sources, then freezes them. The agents reason only over that evidence, and the weighted score is computed by code, not by the model.

    3. A decision brief comes out

      A scorecard for TRL, MRL, patent strength, market accessibility and evidence confidence; a cited report; the main risks and a recommended first step. Export Markdown or PDF, or share the link. A run takes 6 to 15 minutes.

    Your keys, your bill

    Assessments run on your own API keys: one model key (DeepSeek, Qwen, OpenAI or Anthropic) and one search key (Tavily or Serper). Your providers bill you directly, and this service adds nothing. Keys are saved with your account, encrypted, so you add them once; you can remove them at any time.

    What one assessment cost in model fees when it was measured here: about $0.05 with DeepSeek (deepseek-chat, November 2026), $0.10 to $0.25 with Qwen on Model Studio (qwen3.7-plus and qwen3.8-max, October 2026), and about $0.55 with Anthropic (claude-sonnet-5-5, November 2026). Search is billed by the search service: Tavily's free plan includes 1,000 searches a month, and a new Serper account gets 2,500 free queries.

    Get both keys in 3 steps

    Your free first assessment needs neither key. For the assessments after it:

    1. Model key: create an API key at DeepSeek, the cheapest provider measured here. platform.deepseek.com/api-keys
    2. Search key: create an API key at Tavily. Its free plan includes 1,000 searches a month. app.tavily.com
    3. Create an account here, paste both keys, enter deepseek-chat as the model, and start.
