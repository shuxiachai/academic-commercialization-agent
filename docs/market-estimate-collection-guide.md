# Collecting the first real comparable market-estimate pair

This is a **collection checklist**, not a pre-registration, completed human
review, new scoring policy or authorization to buy reports/contact publishers.
It follows the [20-candidate inspection](results-2026-09-12-market-source-followup.md).
No production component consumes these notes.

## Smallest useful next artifact

Obtain one pair with documented valuation boundaries and retain the existing
scope/time/metric negatives. Prefer resolving **P08 (S15/S16, quantum computing
for drug discovery)** first: both publish a 2025 global headline and overlapping
offerings, but that is not yet comparability. P07 is a second lead only if its
hybrid-product and channel accounting can be established and S14's forecast
conflict is separately resolved. Do not replace a hard candidate without
recording the change or describe this convenience selection as held-out.

For each publisher, bind the following to the exact estimate, with report
edition/date, page or heading, public URL and a short permitted excerpt or
own-word note. Keep unavailable fields null and give a reason.

| Required field | What must be known, not inferred from a title |
|---|---|
| Amount and currency | Literal value, million/billion scale, currency; list any conflicting passages separately. |
| Period and time basis | Calendar/fiscal period and whether the number was an observation, estimate or forecast in that edition. The current date cannot relabel an old forecast. |
| Geography | Included/excluded countries and global versus regional total. |
| Market scope | Product/application inclusions and exclusions, not just the headline name. |
| Measurement basis | Annual revenue versus shipment value, financing or cumulative contracts; manufacturer versus retail/channel value and avoidance of double counting. |
| Price basis | Nominal versus constant prices, reference year if constant, and currency conversion assumptions. A report purchase price is not the market's price basis. |
| Shared-input allocation | For P08, hardware used beyond drug discovery, cloud/service attribution and resold/embedded services; for P07, full hybrid-product sales versus cultured-component value. |
| Underlying source family | Whether different publishers reuse the same original statistics; distinct domains alone do not prove independence. |
| Provenance | Report edition and inspected passages; do not overwrite older observations or claim access to a paid report that was not obtained. |

Exact fiscal-period, exchange-rate and coverage details may make the pair
incompatible; this is a valid result. Matching unknown values are never equal
known definitions. At present no such real positive pair has been established.

## Optional message to request public clarification

Prepare or send this only through an appropriately authorized channel. No
message has been sent as part of this work.

> I am comparing publicly reported 2025 global estimates for quantum computing
> in drug discovery in an academic software project. Could you point me to a
> public methodology/definition page for your specific report edition? I need
> to distinguish annual revenue from shipment/contract value, understand the
> allocation of shared quantum hardware and cloud/software services, and confirm
> whether values use nominal or constant prices and which exchange-rate basis.
> I am not requesting confidential underlying data or a paid report. If a
> detail is not publicly available, that clarification is sufficient.

Request explanation, not a preferred answer or an assurance that two estimates
agree. Do not transmit private reports, credentials or reviewer identities.

## When evidence becomes available

1. Save a new dated observation bound to its source; preserve the original
   candidate draft and these unsuccessful inspections.
2. Have a suitable person check the selected original passages and disclose
   actual review/AI usage; lack of a reviewer does not permit inventing a label.
3. Decide each dimension as supported matching, supported different or unknown.
   Record blockers even if another dimension already fails.
4. Only complete compatible independent inputs can enter a real comparison.
   Missing or conflicting inputs stay out of ratio/score calculation.
5. A replacement cap requires a separate versioned protocol, expected outcomes,
   negative controls, baseline-impact analysis and any necessary paid-run
   authorization. Existing calibration belongs to the old formula and cannot
   transfer automatically.

Collecting one positive demonstrates possibility, not precision, calibration,
report improvement or sufficient production authorization. Do not add new Agent
roles, relax guardrails or open extra searches to make this denominator nonzero.
